I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the IT University of Copenhagen, working in the REAL Lab with Kasper Stoy and Andres Faina. My research asks how robots can manipulate objects without grasping them, using soft deformable surfaces, modular hardware, and a mix of geometric control, learning, and system design.

I am currently a visiting PhD researcher at University College London, working on tactile sensing with the CRISP lab. Before my PhD, I studied Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at IISER Bhopal, where I worked on swarm robotics, collective decision-making, and bio-inspired robot systems.

Selected Publications

Scalable Surface-Based Manipulation Through Modularity and Inter-Module Object Transfer

Pratik Ingle, Jorn Lambertsen, Kasper Stoy, and Andres Faina. 2026.
arXiv:2601.21884

A modular soft manipulation platform that coordinates object transfer across fabric-based modules while reducing actuator count from dense arrays to shared-boundary actuation.

Pratik Ingle, Kasper Stoy, and Andres Faina. 2025. IEEE CASE, pp. 1780-1787.
DOI

Shows how simple linear control can position heterogeneous objects on a nonlinear soft surface.
